Vansiora Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Vansiora is a local browser extension for formatting data, generating passwords, and performing encoding, hashing, and encryption tasks.

Vansiora does not collect, sell, transfer, or share user data with third parties. All formatting, password generation, encoding, hashing, and encryption operations are performed locally in the user's browser.

The extension may read the visible content of the current page only to detect raw JSON or structured data and offer to open it in Vansiora. This content is processed locally and is not sent to any external server.

Vansiora may store formatting history and language preferences locally on the user's device using browser storage. This data remains on the user's device and can be cleared by the user.

Vansiora does not use remote code, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data collection services.
